# Domain Rules — Politics and News (high strictness)

Political and news discussion draws strong opinions, and robust disagreement is
expected and protected. But this is also where dehumanising rhetoric and coded hate
concentrate, so the strictness bar is high: inflammatory language aimed at groups is
read carefully.

## Normal in this domain

- Strong criticism of politicians, parties, governments, policies, and institutions,
  including angry or sarcastic criticism.
- Disagreement about immigration, economics, or social policy stated as argument
  about the issue.
- Calling a public figure's actions corrupt, incompetent, or dishonest.

## Flagged in this domain

- Dehumanising language about a group defined by a protected characteristic
  (comparisons to animals, vermin, disease, or "invasion" framing aimed at an ethnic
  or religious group).
- Calls for violence, removal, or exclusion of people based on who they are.
- Coded antisemitic, racist, or xenophobic language (see glossary_dogwhistles) —
  common here and easy to miss.

## Common trap

Do not flag legitimate political anger as hate. "This government is corrupt and I'm
furious" is NORMAL. The line is crossed when criticism of a policy turns into an
attack on a people. Check the dogwhistle glossary before clearing group-directed
political messages.
