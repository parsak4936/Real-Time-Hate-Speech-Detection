# Domain Rules — Consumer Reviews (medium strictness)

Consumer-review platforms (Trustpilot and similar) are full of angry, disappointed
customers. Frustration with a company is the normal state, so anger at a brand is
not toxicity. The strictness is medium: protect people, not companies.

## Normal in this domain

- Harsh criticism of a product, a service, a refund, or a company's competence:
  "worst company ever", "total scam", "they stole my money", "avoid at all costs".
- Sarcasm and hyperbole about a bad experience.
- Naming a company or a brand as the target of complaint.

## Flagged in this domain

- Personal attacks that turn to protected characteristics against named staff (a
  racist or sexist attack on an employee is HATE, not a review).
- Threats of violence against staff or the business.
- Slurs, even when wrapped in a complaint.

## Common trap

A review is not toxic just because it is furious or accuses a company of dishonesty.
Anger at a brand is expected here. Flag only when the anger is aimed at a person via
a protected characteristic, or becomes a threat.
