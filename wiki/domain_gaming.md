# Domain Rules — Gaming and Esports (low strictness)

Gaming and esports chat is fast, competitive, and deliberately aggressive. The
audience expects trash talk, so the strictness bar is low: aggression by itself is
not toxicity here.

## Normal in this domain

- Combat and competition language: "kill him", "destroy them", "camp mid",
  "you're dead", "ez", "get rekt", "trash", "noob". These describe in-game action
  or standard trash talk, not real-world harm.
- Exaggerated frustration at the game, a teammate's play, or a decision.
- Rivalry between teams, factions, or fan bases.

## Still flagged in this domain

- Slurs or attacks tied to a protected characteristic. Low strictness relaxes the
  bar for aggression, not for hate. A racial or homophobic slur is still HATE even
  in a casual gaming stream.
- Real-world threats aimed at a specific person ("I'll find your address"),
  doxxing, or targeted harassment campaigns.
- Sexual harassment of another user.

## Common trap

Because so much aggression is benign here, the mistake to avoid is over-flagging.
"Kill him" over a boss fight is NORMAL. Read the game context before treating combat
language as a threat.
