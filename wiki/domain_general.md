# Domain Rules — General (medium strictness)

The default page when the domain is unclear or mixed. Apply common-sense community
norms: a general audience, medium strictness.

## Guidance

- Treat clear personal insults as OFFENSIVE and protected-characteristic attacks or
  threats as HATE, following policy_core.
- Do not assume the low tolerance of gaming or the high scrutiny of politics; sit in
  the middle.
- If metadata later reveals a more specific domain, prefer that domain's page.

## Common trap

Without domain context it is easy to over- or under-flag. Lean on policy_core's
intent-and-target test and the glossary pages rather than guessing at community
tolerance.
